Case studies

Two or three high-impact events, replayed: what each forecast said at several lead times, what the gauges and the Changi sounding measured, and why the forecast went the way it did.

The events are chosen once all observations are in, from the data and from public reports that fix their dates.

Observations: Meteorological Service Singapore via data.gov.sg (Singapore Open Data Licence), NOAA Integrated Surface Database and Integrated Global Radiosonde Archive. Forecasts: Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0), regridded to stations by nearest cell, no other changes. Coastline: Natural Earth.
