Method

The verification protocol was written before any forecast was scored. These are its main choices.

Reference
Station observations after quality control. Not a reanalysis.
Test period
Valid times 11 February 2024 to 31 October 2026 (UTC).
Forecasts
GFS and ECMWF IFS 0.25° from the Open-Meteo Previous Runs archive, lead days 1, 2, 3, 5 and 7; whole runs from April 2026.
Baselines
Persistence (the observation 24N hours earlier) and climatology (station, month and local hour, 2017 to 2023).
Matching
One grid cell per station, raw model values, no interpolation. Rain summed from 5-minute gauge totals to the hour; an hour counts only when complete.
Intervals
Moving-block bootstrap over days, on paired differences between two forecasts on the same cases. 2000 resamples, 95 percent.

The full protocol, with the evidence behind each choice and a dated change log, is in the repository: docs/verification_protocol.md.

Observations: Meteorological Service Singapore via data.gov.sg (Singapore Open Data Licence), NOAA Integrated Surface Database and Integrated Global Radiosonde Archive. Forecasts: Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0), regridded to stations by nearest cell, no other changes. Coastline: Natural Earth.
