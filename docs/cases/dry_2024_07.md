# Dry spell, 13 to 30 July 2024

Status: draft, 13 November 2026. The numbers come from the replay; the diagnosis is a draft for the author to check against the soundings and the public reports, and to put in his own words.

Window: 13 July 00:00 to 31 July 00:00, Singapore time. Replay: `python -m tropics_scorecard.cases.replay dry_2024_07`.

## What happened

Singapore's first dry spell since 2019: 18 days with island-wide rain under 1 mm a day ([Mothership](https://mothership.sg/2025/03/2024-singapore-weather-events/), [NEA advisory](https://www.nea.gov.sg/media/news/advisories/index/less-wet-weather-expected-in-the-second-half-of-july-2024)).

From the gauges: 1.6 mm network mean over the 18 days. The gauge data alone finds the same dates as the official record, to the day.

## What each forecast said

| Source | Lead day 1 | Lead day 3 | Lead day 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gauges | 1.6 mm | | |
| GFS | 81.4 mm | 94.6 mm | 41.6 mm (archive fault at this lead) |
| ECMWF IFS 0.25 | 21.0 mm | 19.8 mm | 27.5 mm |
| Persistence | 11.7 mm | 12.1 mm | 27.4 mm |
| Climatology | 103.5 mm | | |

Every forecast was too wet. GFS forecast 50 times the observed rain, close to what climatology would say for July, with its largest hours in the late afternoon. ECMWF IFS 0.25 forecast 13 times the observed rain, but a fifth of climatology: it saw that the spell was dry. Persistence, which simply repeats the day before, came closest.

## The sounding

Changi, 12 and 13 July: south-westerly winds below 850 hPa, and dry air at 700 hPa (dew point 6 to 10 degrees below the temperature). A dry layer in the middle of the troposphere suppresses deep convection.

## Diagnosis (draft)

The reports link the spell to drier air and to tropical cyclones over the northern South China Sea drawing the flow away. GFS kept triggering its afternoon convection through the dry layer, day after day: its rain peaked at 17:00 to 18:00 local time, the usual convective hour. That points to convection in the model that responds too little to dry air aloft. IFS damped it much more.

## Failure mode

Afternoon convection triggered in suppressed conditions: persistent false alarms through a dry spell, strongest in GFS. Entered in `docs/failure_modes.md`.
