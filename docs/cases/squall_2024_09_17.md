# Sumatra squall, evening of 17 September 2024

Status: draft, 13 November 2026. The numbers come from the replay; the diagnosis is a draft for the author to check against the soundings and the public reports, and to put in his own words.

Window: 17 September 12:00 to 18 September 06:00, Singapore time. Replay: `python -m tropics_scorecard.cases.replay squall_2024_09_17`.

## What happened

An intense, fast-moving squall line formed over the Strait of Malacca and crossed Singapore between about 19:00 and 20:30, bringing down trees across the island ([Ministry of Sustainability and the Environment](https://www.mse.gov.sg/latest-news/written-reply-to-pq-on-extreme-weather-conditions/), [Mothership](https://mothership.sg/2025/03/2024-singapore-weather-events/)).

From the gauges: in the hour ending 20:00, 60 of 62 gauges recorded rain and 31 recorded 10 mm or more. The network mean for that hour was 10.3 mm, out of 10.8 mm for the whole window. One hour held almost all of it.

No forecast had the squall. GFS was almost dry. ECMWF IFS 0.25 had light rain spread through the window, close to the right total and nothing like the right hour.

## The sounding

Changi, 17 September. At 00 UTC: westerly winds of 12 metres per second at 850 hPa. At 10 UTC, two hours before the squall arrived: the air between 850 and 500 hPa was almost saturated (dew point within 0.2 to 2 degrees of the temperature), with west-north-westerly winds. Moist, westerly flow from the Strait of Malacca: the setting in which Sumatra squalls form.

## Diagnosis (draft)

A squall line is organised convection tens of kilometres across that lives for a few hours. A model with 13 to 28 km cells and parametrised convection cannot form or steer one. What it can do is rain lightly over a wide area in a moist westerly flow, which is what IFS did. The right total with the wrong hour is the mark of that.

## Failure mode

Organised, fast convection: not represented at these grid sizes. An hourly forecast misses it entirely; a daily total can look right by accident. Entered in `docs/failure_modes.md`.
