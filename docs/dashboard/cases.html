Case studies

Three high-impact events, replayed: what each forecast said at several lead times, what the gauges and the Changi sounding measured, and why the forecast went the way it did.

Chosen from the gauge data and public reports before any forecast was looked at. The written diagnosis for each is in the repository, under docs/cases.

Observations: Meteorological Service Singapore via data.gov.sg (Singapore Open Data Licence), NOAA Integrated Surface Database and Integrated Global Radiosonde Archive. Forecasts: Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0), regridded to stations by nearest cell, no other changes. Coastline: Natural Earth.
